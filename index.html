CatchAll Video Downloader

Save videos and audio from the sites you use — on Windows and macOS.

What it does

  • Downloads video and audio from YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, Douyin, X (Twitter), Xiaohongshu and Instagram.
  • Paste a link — title, uploader, duration, available quality and a preview appear automatically.
  • Quality up to 4K saved as MP4, or audio only as MP3 / M4A.
  • Download queue and history with thumbnails; playlists and multi-video posts download as a set.
  • Interface in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Bahasa Melayu and 日本語.
  • No account, no ads, no data collection.
Please download only content you own or are permitted to download, and respect each platform's terms of service. CatchAll is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any of the platforms listed above.
