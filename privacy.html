English

Summary: CatchAll does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to us. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising SDK in the app.

What the app does with data

  • Links you paste are sent only to the video platform they belong to (for example YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) in order to fetch information about the video and download it. They are not sent anywhere else.
  • Downloaded videos, settings, download history and thumbnail previews are stored only on your device (under your user profile's application data folder). You can delete them at any time from within the app or by deleting the folder.
  • Browser cookies are read only if you explicitly choose a browser under "Browser cookies". They are used solely to access the selected site on your behalf, are never uploaded to us or any third party, and are not stored by the app beyond the session.
  • Clipboard: with "Auto-detect copied links" enabled, the app checks the clipboard for video links while it is running; only links from supported sites are used, and nothing is transmitted. You can turn this off.
  • Network connections are made only to the video platforms you use and to their content servers. The Store version does not download or execute any code at runtime; updates are delivered through the Microsoft Store.

Third parties

The video platforms you access are governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service. The app is not affiliated with any of them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

中文

概要：CatchAll 不收集、不存储、也不向我们传输任何个人数据。应用内没有账号、注册、统计分析或广告组件。

应用如何处理数据

  • 你粘贴的链接只会发送给该链接所属的视频平台（例如 YouTube、TikTok、Instagram），用于获取影片信息和下载，不会发送到其他任何地方。
  • 下载的影片、设置、下载历史和缩略图预览只保存在你自己的设备上（用户目录下的应用数据文件夹）。你可以随时在应用内删除，或直接删除该文件夹。
  • 浏览器 Cookie：只有当你在"浏览器 Cookie"里明确选择了某个浏览器时才会读取，仅用于代你访问所选网站，绝不会上传给我们或任何第三方，应用也不会长期保存。
  • 剪贴板：开启"自动读取剪贴板里复制的链接"时，应用在运行期间会检查剪贴板中是否有视频链接；只处理受支持网站的链接，不会传输任何内容。此功能可以关闭。
  • 网络连接仅指向你使用的视频平台及其内容服务器。商店版不会在运行时下载或执行任何代码，更新通过 Microsoft Store 发布。

第三方

你访问的各视频平台受其各自的隐私政策和服务条款约束。本应用与它们没有任何关联。

儿童

本应用不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

变更

如本政策有变更，新版本会连同新的生效日期发布在本页面。
